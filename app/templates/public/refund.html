Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: {{ now_year }}

1. Eligibility

Refund requests are considered within 7 days of purchase, provided you have not accessed more than 10% of the course's study material or mock tests.

2. Non-refundable cases

Once study material has been substantially accessed, mock tests attempted beyond the free preview, or the course validity has expired, the purchase becomes non-refundable.

3. How to request a refund

Email support@kalyaniexamhub.com with your registered email and payment reference (UTR/UPI reference code). Our team verifies eligibility and responds within 3–5 business days.

4. Processing time

Approved refunds are credited to the original payment method within 7–10 business days.

5. Subscription renewals

Course access does not auto-renew; you choose when to renew a subscription, so no refund is due for a lapsed subscription you did not renew.
