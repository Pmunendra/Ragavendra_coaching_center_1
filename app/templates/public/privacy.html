Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ now_year }}

1. Information we collect

We collect the information you provide when registering (name, email, phone, college, address) and information generated while you use the platform, such as test attempts, scores, study material access logs, and payment references.

2. How we use your information

Your data is used to operate your account, grade and analyse your test attempts, generate AI performance insights, process payments and subscriptions, send exam and result notifications, and improve our courses and question banks.

3. Study material protection

Watermarks embedded in study material (your name, ID and access time) and PDF access logs exist solely to protect faculty content from unauthorised redistribution and are not shared with third parties.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Limited data may be shared with payment processors to verify transactions and with email providers to deliver notifications and results.

5. Data security

We use password hashing, session security, rate limiting and authenticated, watermarked delivery of study material to protect your account and our content.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at support@kalyaniexamhub.com.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@kalyaniexamhub.com.
